FAQ

Before you message me

Quick answers to what brands usually ask first. Anything else, just send me a message.

Ask me directly
Do you only edit, or can you make the whole ad?

Both. I can edit your footage and UGC into Meta ads, or produce a full AI ad from your brief: script, storyboard, characters, scenes, voiceover and the final edit.

Can you make ads in other languages?

Yes. Besides English, I've made ads in Danish, Dutch, Finnish, Norwegian and Swedish. Scripts are written to sound native to that market, not like a word-for-word translation.

What kinds of AI ads do you make?

AI UGC, storytime and drama ads, Pixar-style cartoon ads and song ads, all built around your product and edited to feel native to the feed.

What do you need from me to start?

Your product, a few ads or brands you like, and what the ad needs to do. If you already have a brief or script, great. If not, I can write the script and storyboard for you.

What are your hours?

25 to 40 hours a week, Monday to Saturday. I'm based in the Philippines (GMT+8), and I'm looking for brands I can work with long term.
